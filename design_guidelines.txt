Splendor AI: Project Purpose

This project explores training a small neural network to play the board game
Splendor. Its purpose is fun and education: learning how a neural network works,
how it learns to make decisions, and how to understand its behavior.

Learning Comes First

The objective is not to build the fastest model or the strongest Splendor player.
The objective is to understand what we build and why it behaves the way it does.
Keep the project small and approachable, with room to explore one idea at a time.
When performance and understanding compete, prioritize understanding.

Experimentation Is the Work

Try ideas, make mistakes, observe what happens, analyze the results, and try again.
Failed experiments and weak players are useful when they teach us something.
Question assumptions and treat surprising results as opportunities to learn.

What Success Means

Success means being able to explain what we tried, what happened, what we learned,
and what we want to explore next. Playing Splendor gives the learning a concrete
goal, but winning games is secondary to curiosity, understanding, and enjoyment.
